The best movies according to ME!

Dan's TOP TEN

NUMBER 10: Sinister 1

A go to, when people ask me for a horror recommendation.
The right amount of censored gore, great scares, and overall good plot.

NUMBER 9: Wolf of Wall Street

Amazing acting, a story of humble beginnings that really takes a drastic turn.
Aiming high is great, but don't forget to land now and again.

NUMBER 8: Catch me if you can

Amazing story, and great acting again by Leonardo DiCaprio.
The story seems so sureal until you see that this was based on true events.

NUMBER 7: Moana 1

Perfect soundtrack. Compelling story. Tear jerking momemnts.
Accepting responsibility and accepting your flaws. I love this movie so much.

NUMBER 6: The Mummy 1

Classic Brendan Fraser. This movie will always be close to my heart
Mix of action, comedy and horror(kinda). Please do a remaster! (or a part 4)

NUMBER 5: Forrest Gump

Who doesn't love this movie? That's enough said
If you are one of those people who haven't seen it yet, I feel sad for you.

NUMBER 4: Frozen 2

This movie will always be in my top 5. Soundtrack and Story just click with me.
For the love of everything that is good. DO NOT MAKE A PART 3. LET IT END LIKE THIS.

NUMBER 3: Interstellar

What can I not say about this movie that hasn't already been said?
Mint Perfect Soundtrack by Hans Zimmer. Mint Perfect Direction by Christopher Nolan Mint Perfect acting by Matthew McConaughey, Anne Hathaway, Mackenzie Foy and a whole lot more I can't fit here.

NUMBER 2: The Lord of the Rings Trilogy

I know this seems like cheating, including all three movies. Sue me.
The greatest piece of media man has ever produced. PERIOD.

NUMBER 1: Your Name. Kimi no Na Wa

This movie has made me ball out not once, twice, three times. EVERY. SINGLE. TIME. I watch it.
To those that say it's just animation. Give it a try, and if it doesn't make you ball out, give me you bank account number I will personally give you $300.
